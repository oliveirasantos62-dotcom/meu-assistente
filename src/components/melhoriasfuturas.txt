# PROMPT 01 — NEOLIFECITY: MEGACIDADE CYBERPUNK

Create an extremely detailed, massive top-down 2D pixel art city environment for a futuristic life simulation RPG called NeoLifeCity.

**VISUAL DIRECTION**

The aesthetic must resemble a high-quality 16-bit / 32-bit pixel art RPG, combining the visual density of classic top-down cyberpunk games with the charm and exploration of cozy life simulation games.

The city must feel enormous, alive, explorable and full of personality.

**CITY SCALE AND LAYOUT**

- A gigantic urban environment with multiple interconnected districts.
- Dozens of buildings, blocks, alleys, plazas and intersections.
- Dense streets with sidewalks, pedestrian crossings and neon signs.
- Small shops between massive futuristic buildings.
- Rooftops, bridges, elevated walkways and underground entrances.
- Visible environmental storytelling and tiny decorative details.

**BUILDINGS**

Include:
- Futuristic apartments and residential towers.
- Cybernetic technology shops.
- Neon supermarkets and restaurants.
- Universities and research laboratories.
- Gaming arcades.
- Medical centers.
- Robotic workshops.
- Night markets.
- Parks with futuristic vegetation.
- Community centers and entertainment venues.

**ENVIRONMENTAL DETAILS**

- Holographic advertisements.
- Neon shop signs.
- Glowing windows.
- Vending machines.
- Small robots and delivery drones.
- Street lamps.
- Digital billboards.
- Futuristic vehicles.
- Rooftop gardens.
- Pedestrians walking through streets.
- Neon reflections on wet pavement.

**COLOR PALETTE**

Deep navy, dark purple, violet, electric blue, cyan, magenta and occasional warm orange lighting.

**PIXEL ART REQUIREMENTS**

- Strict pixel art aesthetics.
- Crisp pixel edges.
- No blurry textures.
- No 3D rendering.
- Consistent perspective and tile proportions.
- Carefully designed building silhouettes.
- Rich environmental details.
- Professional indie game art quality.

**COMPOSITION**

Show a very large continuous city map from a bird's-eye / top-down perspective, with enough visible streets and buildings to communicate the scale of an entire futuristic metropolis.

The result should look like an actual playable game world, not a poster, concept painting or user interface.

**OUTPUT**

High-resolution image, 16:9 landscape composition, detailed game environment concept art, suitable as a visual reference for creating modular 16x16 and 32x32 pixel art tilesets.

No text overlays, no menus, no HUD, no logos, no watermarks.
